Juno Journey reminder

Requesting paid courses

Paid courses are available through the Juno Journey process, with team review to keep requests aligned to budget and growth priorities.

  1. 1Find the course
  2. 2Submit in Juno
  3. 3Team reviews budget, fit, and timing
  4. 4Complete one approved paid course before another is assigned

Budget is intended for paid courses. Requests are reviewed to align learning with development needs, available budget, and the right timing.

Practice reminder. As practice, the team assigns one paid course at a time.

L.E.A.D. Leadership Coaching Program

From immersive alignment to applied leadership practice

A leadership coaching journey that progresses from immersive alignment to applied practices in decisiveness, creativity, and team growth.

Program overview

The program launched with an immersive retreat in May, then continued through June and July forums focused on practical leadership tools, decision-making, and maximizing each leader’s zone of genius.

01May

Kick-Off Retreat

The Farm at San Benito

  • Arrival & container setting; core foundations
  • Deep dive, feedback, closing, and integration
  • Daily wellness sessions included
02June

Forum Workshop

Decision Making

  • Focused on Fear Melters, F.A.C.T., and decisiveness
  • Fear Melters: Fight, Ooze, Flee, Root, Freeze, Wiggle, Faint, Love Scoops
  • F.A.C.T.: Face, Accept, Choose, Take Action
  • Full Body Yes
03July

Upper Limits Workshop

Commitment 8: Genius

  • Explored zone of genius, flow, and meaningful work
  • Connected conscious leadership with enabling others’ full potential
  • Reinforced creativity, inspired contribution, and growth

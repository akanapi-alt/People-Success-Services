How do I use Career Growth and request paid courses?

How this supports you

  • See what is possible. Explore growth options beyond your current role and understand possible directions for your career journey.
  • Understand expectations. Review the role, skill, and competency expectations that can guide your development priorities.
  • Plan learning with purpose. Make learning choices more intentional and connected to future goals.
  • Support better conversations. Bring clearer context into development check-ins, coaching, and Personal Development Plan discussions.

How to use it

  1. Open JunoGo to My Development > Career Growth.
  2. Explore pathsReview roles and growth directions relevant to your goals.
  3. Plan next stepsUse insights to shape learning, coaching, and PDP priorities.

Juno Journey reminder

Requesting paid courses

Paid courses are available through the Juno Journey process, with team review to keep requests aligned to budget and growth priorities.

  1. 1Find the course
  2. 2Submit in Juno
  3. 3Team reviews budget, fit, and timing
  4. 4Complete one approved paid course before another is assigned

Budget is intended for paid courses. Requests are reviewed to align learning with development needs, available budget, and the right timing.

Practice reminder. As practice, the team assigns one paid course at a time.

L.E.A.D. Leadership Coaching Program

From immersive alignment to applied leadership practice

A leadership coaching journey that progresses from immersive alignment to applied practices in decisiveness, creativity, and team growth.

Program overview

The program launched with an immersive retreat in May, then continued through June to September with workshops focused on practical leadership tools, decision-making, maximizing each leader’s zone of genius, and leading through change.

View the workshop journey
01May

Kick-Off Retreat

The Farm at San Benito

  • Arrival & container setting; core foundations
  • Deep dive, feedback, closing, and integration
  • Daily wellness sessions included
02June

Forum Workshop

Decision Making

  • Focused on Fear Melters, F.A.C.T., and decisiveness
  • Fear Melters: Fight, Ooze, Flee, Root, Freeze, Wiggle, Faint, Love Scoops
  • F.A.C.T.: Face, Accept, Choose, Take Action
  • Full Body Yes
03July

Upper Limits Workshop

Commitment 8: Genius

  • Explored zone of genius, flow, and meaningful work
  • Connected conscious leadership with enabling others’ full potential
  • Reinforced creativity, inspired contribution, and growth
04Aug

Genius vs. Excellence Workshop

Commitment 8: Genius

  • Explored over-functioning as a hiding place from vulnerability and expansion
  • Applied lessons from The Big Leap, 5 Dysfunctions of a Team, and How to Win Friends & Influence People
  • Defined personal commitments to shift from tactical firefighting to strategic influence
05Sept

Change Management Workshop

Leading Yourself Through Change

  • Explored change management and leading yourself through transitions
  • Practiced delegating operational tasks to protect energy instead of “hero mode”
  • Continued peer coaching through Exploring the River, Blind Council, and related formats
